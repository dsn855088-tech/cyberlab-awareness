CYBERLAB AWARENESS LESSON

This was a simulated login page.

The Loopline social network is fictional. This exercise demonstrates how a polished page can look convincing without proving that a service is legitimate.

Check the domain

Look carefully at the full domain name, spelling, and unexpected subdomains before entering information.

Don't trust appearance alone

Brand colors, profile pictures, professional layouts, and padlock icons do not establish that a site is trustworthy.

Use a known route

Open services through a saved bookmark or the official app, not a surprising link in a message.

Pause at the sign-in screen

Unexpected urgency, odd requests, and mismatched domains are reasons to stop and verify.

Privacy by design: this demo never sends or stores the text typed into the username or password fields. It records only generic consented events with a random anonymous session ID. Events are held in memory and may disappear when the service restarts.
